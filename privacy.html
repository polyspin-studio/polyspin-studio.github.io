Privacy Policy for PolySpin

Effective Date: November 2026

PolySpin Studio ("we", "our", or "us") built the mobile game PolySpin as an ad-supported game. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number). Game progress and settings (such as audio and vibration preferences) are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

PolySpin uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain information from your device, such as your Advertising ID, IP address, device type, and app interaction data, in order to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and measure ad performance.

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Your Privacy Choices (GDPR / EEA / UK)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or other applicable regions, you will be asked for your consent regarding personalized advertising. You can review or change your consent preferences at any time via the Privacy options button in the in-game Settings menu.

4. Children's Privacy

PolySpin is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
